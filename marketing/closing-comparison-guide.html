Compare your closing costs. Keep your team.

A practical checklist for homebuyers, real estate agents and loan officers comparing title and settlement providers together.

1. Compare the same closing

Ask for written estimates using the same property, purchase or refinance details, loan amount, coverage and expected closing date. Compare all required service fees together; one low settlement fee may not include the full package.

List insurance premiums, government charges and promotional credits separately. Ask about anything missing. Use the side-by-side worksheet.

2. Confirm that the provider fits your file

Ask your loan officer which services you can shop for and whether the lender can work with the provider you choose. Check your contract and local closing requirements. Confirm the provider's legal entity, service availability, point of contact and ability to meet the deadline.

Read about choosing title services while keeping your agent and lender.

3. Coordinate before placing or changing an order

Find out whether title work has already started. Ask about completed work, incurred charges, document transfer and timing before canceling an existing order. Confirm who will place the new order and share the closing team's contact information.

Questions to send with the estimates

  • Do both quotes include the same services, policies and endorsements?
  • Which amounts are service fees, premiums, government charges or credits?
  • Are any fees missing, estimated differently or bundled into another line?
  • Can the lender accept this provider, and can the team meet our closing date?
