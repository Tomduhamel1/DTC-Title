How the refi number is honestly smaller

Purchase savings include Owner's title insurance ($1,750 on $500k) and a larger Lender's title policy. Refis don't pay for owner's coverage, and lender's title insurance is roughly half-price (lower risk — the lender re-runs diligence on a property they already trust).

So at $500k home value:
— Purchase $500k / $400k loan: ~$9,260 lifetime savings
— Refinance $400k loan (assumes 80% LTV): ~$2,710 lifetime savings

Toggle between modes in any of the three concepts above and watch the number drop ~70%. That's the right shape — refi savings are real, just smaller in absolute dollars.
