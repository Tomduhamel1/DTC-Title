# Iowa — Market Fee Evidence

## Status: complete (saturated) — 6 verified sources, 2026-07-22

Iowa is structurally unlike every other state surveyed to date: **it is the only U.S. state with no
private title insurance market for residential real estate.** This was confirmed (not assumed) via
First American's own "Laws & Customs" reference and Iowa Finance Authority materials — no Stewart,
First American, Fidelity, Old Republic, or WFG rate manuals are filed for Iowa because none of them
underwrite there. Instead, **Iowa Title Guaranty (ITG)**, a state-run program housed in the Iowa
Finance Authority, issues title guaranty certificates at a **flat statewide rate** ($175 up to
$750,000 coverage), and title clearance itself runs through a parallel **abstract-and-attorney
system**: an abstract company certifies/updates the chain of title, an Iowa-licensed attorney
renders a title opinion (title clearance is "the practice of law" in Iowa), and only then does ITG
issue its certificate. This produces a genuinely different evidence shape than other states: 5 of 6
verified sources here are real settlement/service-fee schedules (not premium-only manuals), a rarer
and richer outcome than most states surveyed.

This session ran **49 distinct query strategies and direct provider-site checks**, well past the
8-strategy threshold, and verified **6 good sources**: ITG's residential and commercial pricing
(the state guaranty program itself), one independent abstract/title-guaranty company (Clinton/
Maquoketa), one closing-attorney law firm (Des Moines), and two more independent abstract companies
(Fort Dodge, Ames). Larger Iowa metros — Cedar Rapids, Davenport/Scott County, Iowa City, Sioux City
— were searched specifically but yielded **provider names with no published pricing** (a structural
market feature: those firms operate quote-only or, in one case, behind a signup-gated rate
calculator, not a search-execution gap). The 3 most recently added sources (Hastings & Gartin,
Abstract Associates of Iowa, Abstract & Title Services of Story County) show component prices
consistent with the two already on file (title reports $125-$250, Form 900/901 guaranty-adjacent
fees $175-$225, refinance searches $250-$400) — no wild outliers beyond the well-explained
new-abstract-vs-continuation swing (see below). Marked **complete (saturated)**, with the Cedar
Rapids/Davenport/Iowa City/Sioux City coverage gap flagged as a known limitation rather than grounds
to keep the state open, since exhaustive search confirmed those markets simply don't publish static
pricing online.

## All-in service-stack range observed

A typical Iowa purchase closing stacks: abstracting/title-report fee + attorney title-opinion fee +
settlement/closing fee + the ITG certificate fee ($175 flat, or pass-through as seen at Hastings &
Gartin). Representative all-in totals:

- **Des Moines (attorney-run closing, Hastings & Gartin):** Title Opinion $250 + Settlement Fee $600
  + Deed Prep $100-150 + ITG $175 = **~$1,125-$1,175** (excluding any abstract-update cost, which is
  typically handled by a separate abstract company and not itemized on the attorney's own page).
- **Ames (abstract-company-run, Story County):** routine continuation $550-850 + Title Report $200 +
  Form 900 $200 + Escrow (abstracts/deeds) $250 = **~$1,200-$1,500** for a continuation-based
  closing; a brand-new Root of Title Abstract (rare) pushes this to **~$2,000-$3,200+**.
- **Fort Dodge (Abstract Associates of Iowa):** Lien Search/Title Report $200-250 + Form 900 $200 +
  Post-Closing Certification $200 = **~$600-650** (lower than Des Moines/Ames, though this excludes
  a separate attorney title-opinion fee not itemized on this abstract company's own page).
- **Clinton/Maquoketa (Abstract & Title Guaranty Co.):** Combination Report $150-185 + Title
  Guaranty 900/901 $175-225 = **~$325-410** core stack, one of the leaner combinations found,
  though again excludes a separately-billed attorney opinion.
- The **single largest swing** in the dataset is not cross-provider but within-provider: a new Root
  of Title Abstract ($1,300-$2,500, built from scratch) vs. a routine continuation ($550-850) at
  Story County — an order-of-magnitude difference driven by whether Iowa's abstract-based title
  history already exists for the property, a genuine market characteristic of the abstract system
  rather than provider-to-provider price variation.

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- Every source itemizes **abstracting/title-report work separately from the ITG certificate fee**
  and (where a closing attorney is involved) separately again from the attorney's settlement/
  title-opinion fee — a three-way split (abstract, attorney, guaranty) not seen in states with a
  conventional private-underwriter title insurance market.
- **Form 900 / Form 901** naming is consistent across multiple independent abstract companies
  (Abstract & Title Guaranty Co., Abstract Associates of Iowa, Abstract & Title Services of Story
  County) — these are the standardized pre-closing/post-closing forms that feed ITG certificate
  issuance, priced $175-$225 (Form 900, pre-closing) and $50-$190 (Form 901/post-closing,
  varies more by provider).
- **Refinance pricing is consistently cheaper and simpler than purchase**: ITG's Owner and
  Simultaneous coverage products are Purchase-only (owner coverage persists through a refinance
  without a new certificate); abstract companies charge flat, lower refinance fees ($250-$400)
  vs. new-abstract/continuation costs for a purchase ($550-$2,500).
- **Commercial ITG pricing is a different shape entirely**: percentage-based premium ($1/$1,000,
  $250 minimum) plus flat closing fees ($750 refinance / $1,500-$2,500 purchase / $400 construction
  draw), with third-party abstracting fees passed directly to the abstractor rather than bundled.

## Metro differences

Des Moines (Polk County) and Ames (Story County) both have priced, itemized sources; Clinton/
Maquoketa and Fort Dodge (smaller counties) also publish. Cedar Rapids, Davenport/Scott County,
Iowa City, and Sioux City — Iowa's other major metros — were searched directly (provider names
identified: Republic Abstract & Settlement/Community Title, Iowa Title Company, Hawkeye Title &
Settlement, Engleson Abstract, Sedgwick Talley Abstract/Title Resource Network) but **none publish
static pricing online**; all operate quote-only or behind a gated rate calculator. This appears to
be a genuine feature of how Iowa's larger-metro abstract/title firms operate, not a search gap.
