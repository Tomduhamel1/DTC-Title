## Observed settlement/service-stack range

At a representative $250,001-$300,000 purchase-price tier, the base closing/settlement fee
(before add-on line items like wire, recording, tax service, etc.) across the 8 Chicago-metro
sources that publish one:

| Provider | Closing fee at $250,001-$300,000 tier |
|---|---|
| Greater Illinois Title (2021) | ~$1,680 (computed: $1,580 + $1/$1,000 × 100) |
| Old Republic (2024) | $1,850 |
| First American (2026) | $2,100 |
| TitleStar Cook & Collar (2026) | $2,150 |
| Chicago Title (2026) | $2,150 |
| Fidelity National (2026) | $2,150 |
| Truly Title (2026) | $2,150 |
| Proper Title (2026) | $2,175 |

**Chicago-metro range: ~$1,680-$2,175** (a ~30% spread, driven mostly by rate-card vintage —
the 2026-effective cards cluster tightly at $2,100-$2,175, while the 2021/2024 cards are
noticeably lower). Four of the eight 2026-vintage Chicago-metro cards (Chicago Title,
Fidelity National, Truly Title, and — for the $150k-$500k tiers — closely tracking First
American/TitleStar) publish **numerically identical or near-identical** closing-fee tiers
($1,950/$2,050/$2,100/$2,150/$2,200/$2,250 at the $150k/$200k/$250k/$300k/$400k/$500k
breakpoints), suggesting a shared Chicago-market pricing benchmark rather than four
independently-set schedules.

**Downstate Illinois is dramatically lower**: TitleStar's Central and Southern Illinois
cards both show a flat **$500** closing fee for any transaction up to $300,001 — roughly
one-quarter of the same company's own Cook & Collar Counties tier ($1,950-$2,150) for an
identically-structured transaction. This is the largest single within-company urban/rural
differential found in any state surveyed to date, and is corroborated by the underlying
title insurance premium schedule showing the same pattern (Southern IL owner's policy at
$450,001-$500,000 is $755, vs. Cook & Collar's $2,900 at the equivalent $500,001-$510,000
tier — roughly 4x).

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- **Two-schedule structure is universal**: every Illinois source separates the title
  insurance premium schedule from the closing/escrow fee schedule as two independently
  tiered tables, even when published on the same rate card (e.g., Old Republic, GIT,
  First American, Chicago Title, Fidelity, Proper Title, Truly Title all do this). WFG's
  underwriter manual publishes premium only and explicitly disclaims any closing/escrow
  content — the cleanest statement of this pattern found.
- **Refinance discount converges at 70-75% of the owner's/purchase rate**: Old Republic
  states refinance premium is 75% of owner's pricing; GIT and both TitleStar cards state
  70% "Refinance Package Rate" for the bundled loan-policy-plus-closing-fee product.
- **CPL pricing converges tightly** across the 4 sources that publish it (Old Republic,
  Chicago Title, Fidelity National): purchase-side CPL is uniformly $50 (seller) / $25
  (buyer) / $25 (lender, charged to buyer); refinance CPL is $75 total ($25 lender + $50
  borrower, or split $50/$25 depending on source) — near-identical figures across
  otherwise-competing underwriters and independents.
- **Chicago-specific municipal fees appear on nearly every Chicago-metro card**: a
  "Chicago Water Certification/Processing" fee ($100 across Old Republic, First American,
  TitleStar Cook & Collar, Chicago Title, Fidelity, Truly Title) and a "Chicago Zoning
  Certification" fee ($100-$125) are itemized separately — a city-specific line item absent
  from the Central/Southern Illinois cards, which serve no Chicago-proper properties.
  "IL-APLD Processing Fee" ($95-$100) also recurs across nearly every source — an
  Illinois-specific statutory/regulatory processing charge (Anti-Predatory Lending
  Database), not found in any other state surveyed to date.
- **Multiple-loan-closing surcharges** appear on 6+ sources at similar levels ($200-$350
  per additional lender closing statement), and **after-hours/dry-closing surcharges**
  ($150-$250) are near-universal — both suggest industry-standard add-on categories in
  this market regardless of underwriter or independent status.
- **Simultaneous-issue loan-policy pricing** varies by an order of magnitude between the
  Chicago-metro (~$575-$600) and downstate (~$150-$350) TitleStar cards, tracking the same
  urban/rural differential seen in the closing fee itself.
