# Minnesota (MN) — Market Fee Evidence

## Status: complete (scarce market)

4 verified good sources (2 title-premium-only filed rate manuals: Stewart, WFG; 2 published
settlement/service-fee schedules: CloseAtTitle, Title Services Inc/Rochester). Fewer than the
6-source saturation floor despite an exhaustive search — 20+ distinct query strategies plus
direct provider-site checks (well past the contract's 8-strategy scarce threshold). Marked
**complete (scarce market)**.

## Verified sources

1. **Stewart Title Guaranty Company** — MN Schedule of Charges, effective 2025-07-30
   (last updated 2025-04-28). Filed premium-only rate manual. General Provisions explicitly
   excludes "other miscellaneous charges, which may be made by local attorneys, abstractors,
   settlement agents, or other title insurance companies and agencies" from the manual.
2. **WFG National Title Insurance Company** — MN Manual of Title Insurance Premiums,
   effective 2025-10-15. Filed premium-only rate manual with the sharpest exclusion language
   found: rates are "the total title insurance fees ... but do not include charges for title
   search, surveys, closing services, settlement services, recording fees, other charges, or
   other monies advanced on behalf of an applicant."
3. **CloseAtTitle** — independent title/escrow company serving the Minnesota Metropolitan
   (Twin Cities) area and Wisconsin. Publishes a static fee page (not a calculator) with
   separate buyer-purchase, seller-sale, and refinance figures: Closing Fee $395/$425/$395;
   Title Services/Exam Fee $695 (buyer)/$595 (seller); Doc Prep/Review $225; Courier/Wire
   payoff fee $75/occurrence. Notes county recording/transfer taxes are additional.
4. **Title Services, Inc.** — Rochester, MN; subsidiary of the Dunlap Seeger law firm
   (closing-attorney-affiliated provider type). Published fee calculator page displays static
   dollar figures directly (not gated behind form input): Closing Fee $275, Title Evidence Fee
   $200, Title Examination Fee $235, Recording Service Fee $200, Express Delivery Fee $40, Plat
   Drawing Fee $110, plus government recording fee ($46/document), Mortgage Registry Tax
   (amount x .0023), and Conservation Fee ($5). Page notes figures are "representative of a
   typical transaction."

## Observed service-stack range

Excluding the two premium-only sources (which price title insurance, not settlement
services), the two settlement-fee-publishing sources give an approximate all-in
service-stack range (closing/settlement fee + exam/title-evidence + doc prep/recording where
published, excluding government recording fees and mortgage registry tax) of roughly
**$1,060 (Title Services Inc, Rochester, buyer-side stack: $275 + $200 + $235 + $40 + $200 +
$110) to $1,390 (CloseAtTitle, Twin Cities metro, buyer purchase: $395 + $695 + $225 + $75)**.
This is a genuine urban-vs-smaller-metro differential (~30%) consistent with the pattern seen
in other states (CA, WA, IL) where Twin Cities-area pricing runs meaningfully higher than a
smaller Minnesota metro. With only 2 settlement-fee-priced sources, this range cannot be
tested for saturation (need 6+ to apply the 10%-movement test) — it is reported as observed,
not as a saturated range.

## Provider-type / metro coverage

- **Provider types**: national-brand underwriters (Stewart, WFG — premium only), independent
  title/escrow company (CloseAtTitle), closing-attorney-affiliated title company (Title
  Services Inc, a Dunlap Seeger subsidiary). No national-brand *direct office* (First
  American, Old Republic, Fidelity National, Chicago Title) was found publishing a static
  MN settlement-fee schedule — all route to interactive rate/fee calculators.
- **Metros**: 2 covered — Minnesota Metropolitan/Twin Cities area (CloseAtTitle) and
  Rochester/Olmsted County (Title Services Inc). No Duluth, St. Cloud, or Mankato-specific
  published schedule was found despite targeted searches.
- **Transaction types**: CloseAtTitle publishes separate buyer-purchase, seller-sale, and
  refinance schedules. Title Services Inc's schedule does not distinguish purchase vs.
  refinance explicitly (presented as a general residential closing fee schedule).

## Notable findings

- Minnesota's independent/attorney-affiliated title companies that do publish rates put them
  directly on static web pages rather than behind PDFs — the opposite pattern from most
  filed-rate states surveyed so far, where the underwriters publish PDFs and independents
  publish nothing at all.
- Both Twin Cities-area national underwriters (Stewart, WFG) use nearly identical exclusion
  language disclaiming settlement/closing/escrow fees from their rate manuals, reinforcing
  that MN's title-premium/settlement-fee separation is a consistent market practice even
  though (unlike VA or TN) it does not appear to be a specific statutory mandate cited in
  either filing.
- No independent MN title/escrow or attorney firm outside the Twin Cities/Rochester metros
  (Duluth, St. Cloud, Mankato) was found publishing a static fee schedule — consistent with
  the broader pattern (seen in AZ, CO, MI, MO, VA, WI) of settlement-fee opacity outside major
  metro markets.

## Calculator harvest addendum (2026-07-25)

Separate from the published-schedule survey above (which remains **complete (scarce)**), this
session harvested a first calculator-basis MN provider for the standard $500,000/$400,000 Hennepin
County (Minneapolis) purchase scenario — see MN.json's `"basis": "calculator"` entry and
CALCULATORS.md for full technical detail. **DCA Title** (`dcatitle.com`) runs its own custom
WordPress calculator plugin that computes a genuine itemized quote server-side, reached via a plain
`admin-ajax.php` POST discovered by reading the plugin's own JS source (no browser/JS execution
needed to reproduce). Findings: Closing Service Fee $350, Search & Exam Fee $700 (+$100/additional
parcel), lender's title insurance $1,125, plus MN-specific government charges — Mortgage
Registration Tax $960 (Minn. Stat. 287.035, an ad valorem tax on recorded mortgages unique among
states surveyed to date) and a $5 Conservation Fee. DCA Title's tool also configures Wisconsin, but
WI's county list is not exposed in the static form and every placeholder value tried this session
was rejected server-side — logged in CALCULATORS.md as a follow-up target, not jsOnly (the MN half
works via plain POST). This is 1 of 3 providers needed to cross the calculator-quoted threshold for
MN (see PROGRESS.md tracker) at the time it was harvested. **Update, same session**: a second MN
provider, **Knight Barry Title Group** (`knightbarry.com`, own ASP.NET rate calculator, cracked via
the same `__doPostBack`/`__VIEWSTATE` plain-HTTP replay technique used for FNF and Old Republic
elsewhere in this survey), was also harvested for Hennepin County: Loan Closing Fee $400, Title
Search and Exam Fee $580, Transaction Security Fee $25/side, Conservation Fee $5, plus a notable
disclosed-vs-filed premium distinction (disclosed simultaneous-issue rate vs. the full rate
reported to the underwriter under Minnesota DOC filings). MN now has 2 of the 3 providers needed.

**Update 2026-08-02 — 3rd provider found, MN crosses the calculator threshold.** Harvested
