# Wisconsin (WI) Title / Closing / Settlement Fee Evidence

## Status: complete (scarce market) — 3 verified sources, 2026-07-21

Wisconsin is an insurer-filed title insurance state with a strong attorney-closing culture
(the Attorneys' Title Guaranty Fund, now branded Advocus, historically underwrote policies
issued by member attorneys statewide). Despite 10 distinct query/fetch strategies covering
national underwriters, independent title agencies, and real-estate closing attorneys in
Madison, Milwaukee, and Green Bay, only 3 usable sources were found -- all three are
title-premium-only underwriter rate manuals that explicitly disclaim any settlement,
closing, escrow, or attorney fee content. No independent title company or attorney firm
was found publishing a static settlement-fee schedule; every one found routes to an
interactive calculator, general market-range blog content, or a contact-for-quote page.
Wisconsin is marked **complete (scarce market)**.

## Observed settlement/service-stack range

**None observed.** All 3 verified sources are title-insurance-premium-only rate manuals
that explicitly and specifically disclaim settlement, closing, escrow, attorney, document,
trustee, surveying, and inspection fees as outside their scope. No published Wisconsin
settlement/closing fee schedule was found from any provider type (national underwriter
direct office, independent title agency, or closing attorney) despite an extensive search.
This is a genuine market-opacity finding, not a search failure: Wisconsin's real estate
closings are typically conducted by attorneys (frequently ATG/Advocus-affiliated) or
independent title/escrow agents whose settlement charges are negotiated per-transaction
and quoted directly to clients rather than published as static rate cards -- the same
pattern seen in AZ, CO, MI, MO, and VA.

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- **All three underwriters explicitly disclaim settlement-fee content in nearly identical
  language**: Advocus notes a discretionary agent "Search Fee... not part of the premium
  to be split with Advocus"; Stewart's Definitions table excludes "any settlement fee,
  trustee fee, attorney fee, surveying fee, inspection fee, document fee, closing fee,
  escrow fee or any other fee associated with escrow" from its own "Charge" definition --
  the most complete single-sentence disclaimer list found in any state surveyed;
  First American states outright "This is not a complete Schedule of Fees."
- **Premium schedules converge closely across all three underwriters** at the identical
  rate-tier structure ($575/$525 fixed minimum up to $15,000; +$3.50/thousand to $300,000;
  +$2.50/thousand to $500,000; +$1.00-1.10/thousand to $2,000,000) -- Advocus, Stewart, and
  First American all use nearly the same bracket breakpoints and per-thousand increments,
  suggesting either shared historical rate-bureau origins or close competitive tracking
  in this filed-rate (not promulgated) state.
- **Refinance search-tier pricing (expedited vs. full search) recurs across all three
  underwriters** at similar dollar levels ($500-$525 expedited / $675-$700 full search,
  first $400,000) -- a distinctive Wisconsin-specific structural feature (the "expedited"
  vs. "full search" choice determining whether easements/restrictions get searched) not
  seen as explicitly in other states' rate manuals surveyed to date.
- **Reissue and simultaneous-issue discounting is nearly uniform**: 85% reissue rate
  (Advocus, Stewart, First American all quote exactly 85%) and $500-$575 simultaneous-issue
  loan policy charges across all three -- unusually tight three-way convergence.

## Calculator harvest addendum (2026-07-25)

Separate from the published-schedule survey above (which remains **complete (scarce)** — Knight
Barry's calculator was noted above as "checked but not usable" for that survey since it has no
static published schedule), this session harvested a first calculator-basis WI provider for the
standard $500,000/$400,000 Milwaukee County (WI's most populous county) purchase scenario — see
WI.json's `"basis": "calculator"` entry and CALCULATORS.md for full technical detail. **Knight
Barry Title Group**'s own ASP.NET rate calculator was cracked via a plain `__doPostBack`/
`__VIEWSTATE` HTTP replay (the same technique used for FNF and Old Republic elsewhere in this
survey) — no browser needed. Notable finding: Wisconsin's calculator separately discloses (a) the
rate lenders must show on the federal Loan Estimate/Closing Disclosure forms (higher, per a
WI-specific regulatory rule requiring full owner's-policy-based rates plus the simultaneous-loan
discount) versus (b) the lower "Actual Charges to All Parties" collected at closing — a genuine
regulatory disclosure-vs-reality gap not seen in any other state surveyed. Flat fees: Loan Closing
Fee $350.00, Transaction Security Fee $25.00/side, Recording Fee $30.00/document (stated flat
statewide). This is 1 of 3 providers needed to cross the calculator-quoted threshold for WI (see
PROGRESS.md tracker).
