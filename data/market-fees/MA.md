## Key market structure finding

Massachusetts requires a licensed attorney to conduct real estate closings (no
title-company-only closings), and **title insurance premium rates are NOT filed with
the state** — confirmed independently by a WebSearch synthesis citing Fidelity National
Title's own guidance ("Title Insurance Rates Not Filed in Massachusetts"). This is a
genuinely unregulated/insurer-set-and-unfiled premium market (distinct from insurer-filed
states like ME, WI, KY where premiums are filed but service fees aren't). Because the
closing attorney IS the settlement-fee-charging party in Massachusetts (there is no
separate independent title/escrow company layer as in most other states), attorney
firms' own websites turn out to be the primary published-fee source for this market —
a notably different pattern from most other states surveyed, where closing-attorney
firms were sparse (GA, KY) or entirely silent (VA, AL) on pricing.

## Observed service-stack range (attorney fee only, purchase transactions)

Across the 4 sources, the closing-attorney fee itself ranges **$450 (buyer-only,
dual-representation split) to $1,800 (seller, upper end of Sherman Law's range)**, with
most single-role flat fees clustering **$600-$1,500**. Adding the recurring itemized
extras (title exam $150-$400, plot plan $145-$500, MLC $25-$80, recording $155-$365)
brings an all-in purchase-side service stack to roughly **$1,000-$2,700** depending on
role (buyer/seller/dual) and firm, before title insurance premium itself (quoted by two
firms at ~$4.00/$1,000 of purchase price).

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- Every firm treats the **attorney fee** as the core "settlement fee" equivalent — there
  is no separate independent settlement/escrow company fee layer in this market.
- **Title exam**, **plot plan/Mortgage Inspection Plan**, and **Municipal Lien
  Certificate** are consistently itemized as separate line items outside the flat
  attorney fee across all 4 firms — a much more granular and consistent itemization
  pattern than most other states surveyed.
- **Recording fees** are consistently broken out by instrument (deed, mortgage, MLC,
  homestead) rather than bundled into one number — Lazan and Cote's figures match almost
  exactly (deed $155, mortgage $205, MLC $80, homestead $35), suggesting these are
  Registry of Deeds statutory fees rather than firm-set prices, corroborated across two
  independent firms in different regions.
- **Title insurance premium** is quoted by two firms as a simple $4.00/$1,000 flat rate,
  consistent with the unfiled/insurer-set premium market noted above.

## Premium rate cards

**Verified: Stewart Title Guaranty Company**, `public.stewart.com/vu/rate-book-massachusetts.pdf`
(document copyright "© 2021 Stewart"; recovered via the PDF binary-read technique after
WebFetch itself returned corrupted text). Standard Owner's Policy: $146 minimum
($1,000-$39,000), then $3.65/thousand up to $1M, $2.50/thousand $1M-$2M. Standard Loan
Policy: $100 minimum, then $2.50/thousand up to $2M. Enhanced Owner's: $160 minimum,
$4.00/thousand up to $1M, $2.75/thousand $1M-$2M. Enhanced Loan: $110 minimum,
$2.75/thousand up to $2M. Simultaneous issue fee: $175 flat, added to the Owner's
premium. Refinance rate: 40% credit off the regular loan policy premium. Full rate
tables run $1,000 to $2,000,000 in $1,000 increments. Contains zero settlement/closing/
escrow dollar figures — premium only, consistent with the market-structure finding.

This 2021-vintage document is evidently NOT the same manual as the one referenced by a
Sherman Law blog post (`timshermanlaw.com/ma-rate-manual-2025-what-you-need-to-know/`),
which describes a newer Stewart manual effective 2025-09-15 with different rates (owner's
$4.25/$1,000 min $170 enhanced $4.75/$1,000 min $190, CPL $35) — the 2025 vintage's
actual PDF could not be located this session despite retrying the virtualunderwriter.com
media-path pattern (that pattern's public search results only surfaced other states'
manuals) and a direct search for "stewart title massachusetts rate manual pdf effective
2025". The blog's 2025 figures remain an unverified secondary characterization, not
independently confirmed against a primary document.

WFG's Massachusetts premium rate PDF (`wfgunderwriting.com/wp-content/uploads/filebase/
massachusetts/rates/WFG Massachusetts Rates Effective 3-1-17.pdf`) 404'd on direct fetch

## Calculator harvest addendum (2026-07-26)

Separate from the published-schedule survey above (which remains **complete (scarce

**2 calculators harvested** -- below the 3-provider "calculator-quoted" threshold, reported
honestly as a thin but real result, consistent with MA's mandatory-attorney-closing,
premiums-not-filed market structure already documented above (the same opacity pattern that
kept the published-schedule survey to 5 sources).

1. **Absolute Title, LLC** (`absolutetitle.com`) -- "New England's Premier Title Company,"
   an independent title company with an MA office in Lowell (Middlesex County), though its
   calculator itself has no county selector (statewide MA pricing). A genuine first-party,
   non-platform JS calculator (`rc-ma.js`) with fee logic as literal hardcoded constants/
   formulas -- reproduced directly in Python, no browser execution needed. Settlement Fee
   $850, Plot Plan $150, Lender Premium $1,000, Owner Premium (incremental) $1,300, Wire
   $50 (static), CPL $35 (static).
2. **The Law Office of David R. Rocheford, Jr., P.C.** (`thebestclosings.com`), a Leominster
   (Worcester County) closing attorney -- the richest single itemization found this session.
   A WordPress "title-loan-calculator" plugin combining static per-line fee constants in the
   page HTML with a small JS formula file for the 3 computed lines (owner's/lender's title
   insurance, transfer tax). Buyer side: Settlement Fee $565, Title Exam $300, Survey $160,
   Municipal Lien Certificate $50 (+ $85 recording-side MLC), Overnight $50, Wire $75,
   Recording (Deed & Mortgage) $370, Homestead $40, Owner's Title Insurance $1,075,
   Lender's Title Insurance $1,100 -- Grand Total Buyer Pays $3,870. Seller side: Discharge/
   Release Fee $110 (+ $110 recording-side discharge), Overnight $50, Wire $75, Transfer Tax
   $2,280 -- Grand Total Seller Pays $2,625. This is a direct confirmation of MA.md's
   market-structure finding above: the closing attorney IS the settlement-fee-charging
   party, and this firm's own calculator discloses that fee alongside every ancillary
   government/service charge with buyer/seller allocation.

Both finds followed the "small independent provider's own hand-rolled JS calculator"
pattern already identified as high-yield in CALCULATORS.md (the Modern Title Group/MI
precedent) rather than a shared national platform -- consistent with MA's mandatory-
attorney-closing structure, where no independent title/escrow layer exists to run a
