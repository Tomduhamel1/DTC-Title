# Wyoming — Market Fee Evidence

## Status: complete (scarce market) — 3 verified sources (all premium-only rate schedules), 2026-07-22

Wyoming is a filed-rate title insurance market (the Wyoming Division of Insurance's Rate & Form
Filings process, referenced on doi.wyo.gov/companies, routes through the NAIC's SERFF portal —
serff-sfa.naic.org/serff/sfa/home/WY — which returned HTTP 403 Forbidden on direct fetch this
session, the same interactive-search-not-static-repository pattern documented in prior sessions for
other states' SERFF portals). Wyoming's residential closings are handled by title companies rather
than attorneys (confirmed via search and via Wyoming Title & Escrow's own Homebuyer's/Home
Seller's Handbooks, which describe an escrow-officer-run closing process). This session found and
verified **3 title-premium rate schedules** — Stewart Title Guaranty (statewide), Wyoming Title &
Escrow/First American (Jackson & Thayne, Teton & Lincoln Counties), and Black Hills Title
(Newcastle, Weston County) — but **33 distinct query strategies** plus direct checks of all 5 major
national-brand underwriters/direct offices (First American, Fidelity National/Chicago Title, Old
Republic, Stewart, WFG) and roughly a dozen named Wyoming-specific independent title/escrow
companies (Wyoming Title & Escrow, Black Hills Title, Sovereign State Title, Wilcox Abstract &
Title, Sheridan County Title Insurance Agency, American Title Agency, Eastern Title, TownSquare
Title of Wyoming, Northern Title, Flying S Title & Escrow, Laramie County Abstract & Title/Title
Financial Corporation) found **zero published settlement, closing, escrow, doc-prep, search/exam,
or courier/wire fee dollar figures** anywhere. Marked **complete (scarce)** — far past the
8-strategy threshold, well below the 6-source saturation floor.

## All-in service-stack range observed

No settlement/closing-service-fee range could be established this session. All 3 verified sources are
title-insurance-**premium**-only rate schedules; Stewart's manual explicitly and affirmatively excludes
"settlement fees, trustee fees, attorney fees, surveying fees, inspection fees, document fees, closing
fees, escrow fees" from its defined "Charge," and neither the Wyoming Title & Escrow/First American
partial rate schedule nor the Black Hills Title rate sheet contains a single non-premium dollar figure.
Wyoming Title & Escrow's own Home Seller's and Homebuyer's Handbooks (both fetched and read in
full this session) name "Escrow Fees" as a distinct closing-cost line item and describe the escrow
officer's duties in detail, but the handbooks are purely descriptive glossaries with zero dollar figures
anywhere in either 20-page document — confirming that WY escrow fees exist and are charged, but are
not published. No secondary-source blog commentary with specific figures was found credible enough
even to flag as unverified; general search-engine summaries cited only broad "$1,200-$2,500" and
"2%-5% of purchase price" all-in closing-cost ranges with no attributable provider source, so none of it
is recorded as evidence per the task's evidence rules.

**Title insurance premium (all 3 verified sources), owner's policy at $200,000 of coverage:**
Stewart (statewide) = **$884** (Basic Schedule of Charges: $240 minimum + per-bracket increments
to $884 at $200k), Wyoming Title & Escrow/First American (Jackson/Thayne) = **$840**, Black Hills
Title (Newcastle) = **$884** — Stewart and Black Hills Title's numbers converge almost exactly at
this tier despite being two entirely independent filings by different companies, while the
Jackson/Thayne First American agent runs about 5% cheaper. At $500,000: Stewart = **$1,690**
(interpolated from the per-bracket schedule), WTE/First American = **$1,590**, Black Hills Title =
**$1,694** — the same pattern holds, with WTE/First American consistently the cheapest of the three
by a modest single-digit-percent margin.

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- **All 3 sources price title premium only.** None publish a settlement, escrow, closing, doc-prep,
  processing, courier/overnight, wire, edoc, eRecording, notary/signing, binder, or CPL fee anywhere.
  This is a genuine market-opacity finding (not a search-coverage gap): a dozen named WY
  independent title/escrow companies were checked directly and every single one either has no
  pricing page at all, routes to a login-gated customer portal (Wyoming Title & Escrow's "WTE
  Companion" at companion.wyomingtitle.com), or is blocked outright (see below).
- **Stewart's manual is the most detailed and explicitly excludes settlement/escrow charges** in its
  own General Provisions and Definitions sections — the clearest self-declared premium/settlement
  split found in this session, textually similar to the exclusion language documented in prior "scarce"
  states (WV, KY, ME, MS, AR, ND, NH).
- **Black Hills Title's Wyoming rate table is structurally identical to its South Dakota rate table**
  (verified against the SD.json entry from a prior session) — same three-column Owner's/Extended-
  Lender/Simultaneous-Lender format — but the dollar figures themselves are a separately filed,
  non-identical Wyoming-specific table, confirming the company files distinct rates per state despite
  using one shared table template.
- **Wyoming Title & Escrow (a Mother Lode Holding Company/Placer Title Company subsidiary,
  the same corporate family as Montana Title & Escrow and Washington Title & Escrow verified in
  prior MT/WA sessions) is explicitly labeled a "PARTIAL Rate Schedule"** by its own footer
  disclaimer, and confirms in writing that recording fees, mortgage registration tax, and conservation
  fees are excluded — i.e., even this document does not claim to be a complete fee picture.
- **No standalone CPL (Closing Protection Letter) fee was found** in any of the 3 sources — Stewart's
  manual has no CPL section at all (unusual; most other states' Stewart manuals reviewed in this
  project include one), and neither independent agent's rate sheet references CPLs.

## Metro / regional differences

Coverage spans 2 distinct WY regions with published premium pricing, plus one statewide schedule:
- **Statewide (Stewart Title Guaranty):** single Basic Schedule of Charges with no county/metro
  tiering — the only one of the 3 sources with true statewide reach.
- **Jackson Hole/Teton County & Star Valley/Lincoln County (western WY, Wyoming Title &
  Escrow/First American):** the most detailed, granular rate table found this session ($5,000
  increments from $100,000 to $1,000,000), reflecting the Jackson resort real estate market's
  transaction volume.
- **Weston County/Newcastle (northeastern WY, Black Hills Title):** the only verified source outside
  the Jackson Hole corridor, giving the survey genuine east-west geographic spread even though all
  three sources are premium-only.

No published pricing was found for Wyoming's other major population centers specifically named in
the task brief — **Cheyenne/Laramie County** (Wyoming's capital and largest city; TownSquare Title,
Laramie County Abstract & Title/Title Financial Corporation, and First American's Laramie County
office were all checked, none publish figures), **Casper/Natrona County** (American Title Agency,
Eastern Title, and First American's Natrona County office were all checked, none publish figures), and
**Sheridan** (Sovereign State Title, Wilcox Abstract & Title, and Sheridan County Title Insurance
Agency were all checked, none publish figures — Wilcox Abstract's site was additionally unreachable,
returning HTTP 503 on two separate attempts).

## Premium rate card (filed-rate state)

Wyoming is confirmed insurer-filed for title premiums via the Wyoming Division of Insurance's Rate &
Form Filings process (doi.wyo.gov/companies, "Rate & Form Filings" section linking to
/companies/pc-forms, which is itself only a filing-checklist page, not a searchable document
repository) with a linked NAIC SERFF portal search tool (serff-sfa.naic.org/serff/sfa/home/WY) —
this session's direct fetch of the SERFF URL returned HTTP 403 Forbidden, matching the same
interactive-search-interface-not-a-static-document-listing pattern found in prior sessions for other
states' SERFF portals (e.g., South Dakota, Rhode Island). Virtual Underwriter's own Wyoming bulletin
index confirms a **2025-08-25 Stewart rate-filing update** (bulletin WY2025002: "The Wyoming
Division of Insurance has approved Stewart Title Guaranty Company's latest rate submission, and the
new rates are effective August 25, 2025") — the identical effective date as Black Hills Title's own WY
rate sheet, suggesting a possible statewide rate-cycle coincidence — but the actual updated Stewart
PDF could not be located at any working public URL this session (the public stewart.com Wyoming
rates page still links only to the 2016-vintage manual), so the 2016 filing remains the only verifiable
Stewart Wyoming document. At $200,000 of owner's-policy liability: Stewart (2016 vintage, statewide)
= **$884**, WTE/First American (Jackson/Thayne) = **$840**, Black Hills Title (Newcastle) = **$884**
— see WY.json for the full per-provider tier tables.
