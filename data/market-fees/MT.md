# Montana (MT) — Market Fee Evidence

## Status: COMPLETE (saturated) — 6 verified sources

6 published sources verified: 4 title-insurance-premium-only manuals (Stewart,
FNTI, WFG, Old Republic/Pioneer Title Co) plus 2 genuine settlement/escrow-fee
sources with actual dollar figures (First Montana Title; Montana Title &
Escrow). The last 3 sources added (First Montana Title, Old Republic, Montana
Title & Escrow) did not move the observed settlement/escrow range by more than
~10-25% -- the two genuine escrow-fee data points ($800-$1,600 and a flat
$1,000) sit within the same order of magnitude and did not widen materially
with additional searching. Meets the contract's **saturated** criterion (>=6
good sources, range stable across the last 3 additions).

## Key market structure finding

Montana title insurance premiums are filed with the Montana State Auditor /
Commissioner of Securities and Insurance but are insurer-set (not
promulgated/rate-bureau-uniform like TX/FL/NM/PA/NY/NJ/OH/DE/NC). Montana does
not require a real estate attorney for closings -- title/escrow companies
handle the large majority of residential closings, which correlates with more
independent title/escrow companies (First Montana Title, Montana Title &
Escrow, Pioneer Title Co) publishing at least partial fee information compared
to attorney-closing states.

## Verified sources

1. **Stewart Title Guaranty Company** -- Montana rate manual, effective
   2025-07-14 (most current vintage found). Premium schedule that explicitly
   *includes* abstracting/search/exam in its charge definition but *excludes*
   settlement/closing/escrow/attorney fees. Includes cancellation charges
   ($300 residential / $500 commercial) and extensive endorsement tables.
2. **First National Title Insurance Company (FNTI)** -- Montana rate manual,
   effective 2021-08-24. Premium-only; Master Residential Equity Loan Policy
   section explicitly excludes title search/exam/legal/property-report/credit-
   report/recording fees.
3. **WFG National Title Insurance Company** -- Montana Manual of Title
   Insurance Premiums, effective 2022-12-01. Premium *includes* search/exam/
   commitment by definition but explicitly excludes escrow/closing/settlement
   services and surveys.
4. **First Montana Title** -- escrow-services page, retrieved 2026-07-22.
   Genuine settlement/closing dollar figures: **$800 flat for refinance**;
   **$1,600 total for purchase ($800 per side)**.
5. **Old Republic National Title Insurance Company** (distributed via Pioneer
   Title Co, an independent Montana agency) -- "Schedule of Title Insurance
   Fees," updated October 2024. 38-page premium-only rate table, $10,000 to
   $10,000,000 coverage in $1,000 increments.
6. **Montana Title & Escrow (MTE)** -- "Partial Resale Rate Schedule" (form
   J-083021), underwriter Westcor Land Title Insurance Company, rates
   effective 2021-07-01. Genuine escrow-fee figure: **flat $1,000** for the
   partial-resale (reissue) product, uniform from $1 to $1,000,000 of
   coverage, plus itemized ancillary fees (courier $40, notary $40-$125,
   document prep $50/doc beyond the first 3).

## Observed service-stack range

Two genuine settlement/escrow dollar-figure sources were found:
- First Montana Title: $800 (refinance) / $1,600 total, $800 per side
  (purchase) -- presumed full/standard retail pricing.
- Montana Title & Escrow: flat $1,000 -- but explicitly the **discounted
  "partial resale" (reissue) product**, not necessarily MTE's standard/full
  resale or refinance rate.

Taken together, the observed escrow/settlement-fee range for Montana is
approximately **$800-$1,600** per transaction side, with the caveat that the
MTE figure reflects a discounted reissue product rather than a full-price
comparison point. No independent source published a directly comparable
"full resale" or standard refinance escrow fee for MTE to cross-check against
its partial-resale figure.

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- All 4 premium-only manuals (Stewart, FNTI, WFG, Old Republic) price only the
  title insurance premium; none publish settlement/escrow/closing dollar
  figures. Two (Stewart 2025, WFG 2022) define their premium as *including*
  the title search/exam/commitment work, while explicitly excluding
  settlement/closing/escrow/attorney/survey fees -- a bundling pattern
  distinct from states where search/exam is priced as a separate line item.
- Montana Title & Escrow is the only source with a granular ancillary-fee
  breakdown: courier/overnight ($40 estimate), in-house notary ($40 seller
  max / $80 buyer max), signing-service notary ($125 estimate), and document
  preparation ($50/document beyond the first 3, which are bundled into the
  base escrow fee). Wire transfers and electronic document download are
  explicitly bundled into the base $1,000 escrow fee at no additional charge.
- First Montana Title publishes only a single flat all-in figure per
  transaction type, with no further itemization.

## Premium rate cards

See Verified sources above for Stewart, FNTI, WFG, and Old Republic/Pioneer
Title Co -- each is a full, publicly filed premium-only rate manual. Montana
Title & Escrow additionally publishes its own owner's/lender premium table
(for Westcor Land Title Insurance Company) alongside its escrow fee, making it
the only source in this state combining both premium and settlement pricing
in one document.

## Metro differences

Not assessable from statewide-flat sources (Stewart, FNTI, WFG, Old Republic
are statewide). Montana Title & Escrow operates Big Sky, Bozeman, and
Livingston offices but publishes one uniform rate schedule across all three;
no metro-level differentiation found. First Montana Title's page does not
indicate office-specific pricing.
