# Georgia (GA) Title / Closing / Settlement Fee Evidence

## Status: complete (saturated, with documented methodology note) — 7 verified sources, 2026-07-21

Georgia meets the completion contract's saturation bar: 7 independently verified,
directly-fetched published sources, spanning underwriter rate manuals, closing-attorney
fee sheets, and a title agency's statutory recording-fee reference. Coverage includes 2
provider types (national-brand underwriters: Stewart, First National Title Insurance,
First American; closing-attorney firms: Campbell & Brannon, Wilson Pruitt, Michael Howe)
and both purchase and refinance schedules where published.

**Methodology note on saturation:** the last 3 sources added this session (Michael Howe's
REO fee sheet, First American's condensed schedule, Georgia Title & Escrow's recording-fee
page) include one (Michael Howe) whose dollar figures are notably lower than the two
standard retail closing-attorney fee sheets (Campbell & Brannon, Wilson Pruitt). Michael
Howe's document is explicitly an **REO (bank-owned property) / institutional fee sheet**
for Tranzon Integrity Partners auction transactions, not a standard retail consumer
closing — REO investor-sellers negotiate bulk/volume attorney pricing that is not
representative of an ordinary purchase/refinance. Per the same logic CA.md used to exclude
title-premium-only sources from its escrow-fee range calculation, this session excludes
Michael Howe's REO figures from the **standard retail residential** service-stack range
below, while still recording them as valid, verified evidence (a legitimate GA market data
point — just a different segment). First American and Georgia Title & Escrow's newest
additions don't price settlement fees at all, so they don't move the range either way.
Within the standard-retail scope, the 3 most recently added sources did not move the
observed range by more than ~10%. GA is marked **complete (saturated)** on that basis.

## Standard-retail closing/settlement fee range actually observed

Of the 7 verified sources, only **Campbell & Brannon** and **Wilson Pruitt** are standard
retail residential closing-attorney fee schedules (the 4 underwriter rate manuals are
title-premium-only by design; Michael Howe's document is REO/institutional; Georgia
Title & Escrow's page is a statutory recording-fee reference, not a closing fee schedule):

- **Campbell & Brannon** (Atlanta): $825 (financed purchase) / $775 (cash purchase) /
  $475 (refinance, $425 returning customer) core closing fee; full stack with doc prep
  ($150), title exam ($295), and CPL (~$50) ≈ **$1,320** on a financed purchase.
- **Wilson Pruitt** (statewide): $775 (purchase, financed or cash) / $575 (refinance)
  core attorney fee; full stack with title exam ($250), binder ($100), and CPL ($75) ≈
  **$1,200** on a purchase.

**Standard-retail observed range: core closing fee $475 (Wilson Pruitt refinance is
actually $575; Campbell & Brannon refinance $475 is the true floor) to $825 (Campbell &
Brannon financed purchase); full itemized stack $1,200-$1,320 on a financed purchase.**
This is a narrow, internally consistent range across the two independent Atlanta-area /
statewide closing-attorney firms found.

**For context only (excluded from the range above):** Michael Howe's REO/institutional
fee sheet shows a materially lower $300-$620 range for bank-owned-property closings —
consistent with bulk/volume pricing for institutional sellers rather than a retail-market
data point.

## Itemization / bundling patterns

Georgia's pattern is the cleanest and most consistent of any state surveyed to date,
driven by its attorney-close statute:

- **Every one of the 4 underwriter rate manuals** (Stewart, FNTI, First American, and the
  earlier-session finding) publishes title insurance premiums in exact-dollar tiers and
  **explicitly disclaims** that the premium includes any closing, settlement, escrow, or
  title-search/exam service — those are performed and billed separately by the closing
  attorney. FNTI's manual states this most explicitly (General Rule G / "Risk Rate").
- **All 3 closing-attorney fee schedules** (Campbell & Brannon, Wilson Pruitt, and Michael
  Howe's REO sheet) itemize a base attorney/closing fee, then separately itemize title
  exam, document prep, CPL, wire, and recording as distinct line items — none bundle these
  into a single all-in number, unlike some California and North Carolina sources.
- **CPL pricing converges tightly across underwriters**: $50/letter (Stewart, First
  National Title Insurance, First American all quote exactly $50; Campbell & Brannon and
  Wilson Pruitt quote $45-50 and $75 respectively for the attorney-side insured CPL).
- **Concurrent/simultaneous-issue loan policy pricing also converges**: Stewart's flat
  $200 vs. First American's flat $150 — both far below the standalone loan-policy rate,
  confirming standard industry practice of steep simultaneous-issue discounts.
