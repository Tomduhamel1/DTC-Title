# Idaho — Market Fee Evidence

## Status: complete (saturated) — 8 verified documents, 2026-07-22

Idaho is unusual among filed-rate states surveyed to date: **IDAPA 18.05.01.022 requires every
licensed Idaho title/escrow agent to file its escrow (settlement/closing) rate structure with the
Idaho Department of Insurance annually** (filed by March 15, effective April 15), separately from
the title insurance premium filed under Idaho Code §41-2707. The Department of Insurance publishes
the *compiled result* of that filing requirement as two public statewide-by-county tables — short-
term (standard purchase closing) escrow rates and long-term (contract/installment) escrow rates.
This single regulator-published pair of documents discloses genuine, dated, dollar-denominated
escrow-fee structures (Base Rate + Rate per $1,000 + Minimum Rate) for **~15 distinct licensed title
agents** across Idaho's five major metro counties (Ada/Boise, Kootenai/Coeur d'Alene, Bonneville/
Idaho Falls, Twin Falls, Bannock/Pocatello) — a materially richer and more reliable settlement-fee
evidence base than the market-opacity pattern seen in most other "scarce" states (AZ, CO, MI, MO,
VA, WI, AL), where independents publish nothing and national brands push interactive calculators
only.

Combined with 6 underwriter-filed title insurance premium manuals (First American x2 distribution
channels, Stewart x2 vintages, Old Republic x1, WFG x1), this session verified **8 distinct fetched
documents**, comfortably past the 6-source saturation floor. Because the two DOI filings are a
near-census of Idaho's licensed title agents (mandatory filing, not a voluntary sample), the
observed escrow-fee range is already the *comprehensive* market range rather than a partial
sample — further searching turned up only additional underwriter premium manuals (which explicitly
exclude escrow/settlement charges by disclaimer in every case) rather than new settlement-fee data
points, so the range is not expected to move with further search. Marked **complete (saturated)**.

## All-in service-stack range observed

Escrow fee = Base + (Rate per $1,000 of price) subject to a filed Minimum. At a representative
**$300,000 purchase price**:

- **Ada County (Boise)** — remarkably convergent: nearly every provider (Alliance, Empire, Fidelity
  National, First American, Pioneer, Stewart, TitleFact, TitleOne, Venture) computes to **$1,050**,
  whether via a $150 base + $3.00/1000 formula or a bare $3.50/1000 formula with no base — different
  formula shapes landing on the same total.
- **Kootenai County (Coeur d'Alene)** — tighter but not identical: **$900** (Ticor Title, no base) to
  **$1,050** (Alliance Title, NexTitle) at $300,000.
- Filed **minimums** (the floor at low price points) vary far more by county than by provider:
  **$150-$150** in Ada, **$200-$500** in Kootenai, **$300-$500** in Bonneville/Twin Falls/Bannock —
  i.e., Boise-metro escrow pricing is both cheaper and more standardized than the smaller/more rural
  metros, the inverse of the "urban premium" pattern seen in some other states' data.
- Long-term/contract escrow (a different product, not a standard purchase closing) is priced far
  lower: $50-$75 base, $0.75-$3.00/1000, $75-$250 minimum — recorded for completeness but excluded
  from the standard-purchase range above.

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- Idaho's escrow filings are **pure base+per-thousand+minimum formulas**, not itemized line-item
  breakdowns (no separate doc prep / courier / wire / notary figures disclosed) — the filed number
  is an all-in escrow/closing fee.
- Every underwriter premium manual (First American, Stewart, WFG) contains **explicit disclaimer
  language excluding escrow/settlement/closing charges** from the title premium, confirming Idaho
  follows the standard premium/settlement-fee separation pattern even though, unusually, the
  settlement side is *also* filed with a regulator.
- CPL (Closing Protection Letter) pricing is standardized at **$25.00 per transaction/issuance**
  across both Stewart and WFG.
- Premiums have **converged across underwriters over time**: First American and Stewart's current
  (2024-2026) Owner's Policy rate at $100,000 liability are now identical ($688), while Old Republic
  sits lower ($661) and WFG's 2017 vintage (oldest located) implies an even older, lower tier — real,
  persistent inter-underwriter premium variation despite Idaho's filed-rate structure.

## Metro differences

Boise (Ada County) has the lowest and most standardized escrow-fee minimums ($150 flat across
nearly every filer); Coeur d'Alene (Kootenai), Idaho Falls (Bonneville), Twin Falls, and Pocatello
(Bannock) all file materially higher minimums ($200-$500), suggesting either higher per-transaction
cost structures or less competitive pressure outside the Boise metro. Per-thousand rates are fairly
uniform statewide ($3.00-$3.50), so the minimum-fee floor is the main geographic differentiator at
lower price points, while the per-thousand rate dominates at higher price points.
