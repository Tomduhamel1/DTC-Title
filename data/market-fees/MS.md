## Status: COMPLETE (scarce market) — 2 verified sources

2 premium-only rate manuals verified (Stewart, WFG), both explicitly excluding

## Key market structure finding

Mississippi title insurance premiums are **not regulated/state-approved** — each
insurer files its own rate manual with the Mississippi Insurance Department, but rates
are not filed-and-approved the way TX/FL/NM/PA/NY/NJ/OH/DE/NC are (confirmed both by
web search commentary and directly by Fidelity National Title's own "Real Estate Laws
& Customs" reference, which lists Mississippi rates as "Not filed" / "Negotiable").
Closings are handled by a mix of attorneys and title companies — Mississippi does not
exclusively require either (title companies are typically attorney-owned or
attorney-affiliated to avoid unauthorized-practice-of-law issues), which appears to
correlate with a lack of published settlement-fee schedules from either provider type.

## Verified sources

1. **Stewart Title Guaranty Company** — Mississippi "Schedule of Charges and Forms,"

   surveys, escrow, closing services, settlement services, recording fees, other
   charges, or other monies advanced on behalf of an applicant").

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- Both underwriters use near-identical premium rate structures: Owner's/Leasehold
  $4.00/thousand (first $1M) + $2.00/thousand (excess), Loan $3.00/thousand (first $1M)
  + $1.50/thousand (excess), $150 minimum on both. This convergence (identical to the
  cent) is a notable cross-underwriter corroboration despite MS being formally
  unregulated/insurer-set.
- Both charge an identical $50.00 CPL fee — WFG's manual adds the specific detail that
  the fee is "remitted entirely to the Company" and "not shared by an agent."
- Both explicitly and affirmatively state their premium rates exclude settlement,
  closing, escrow, and attorney fees — matching the exclusion language pattern seen in
  most other insurer-filed states.
- A newer Stewart Mississippi rate manual revision was found to exist (bulletin
  MS2025001, "Mississippi Rate Manual [Revised 11-14-25]," effective 2025-11-17) via
  Virtual Underwriter's Mississippi bulletin index, but the actual revised PDF is
  access-restricted to authorized Virtual Underwriter accounts and could not be
  fetched; the verified 2018-vintage Stewart document remains the evidence of record.
