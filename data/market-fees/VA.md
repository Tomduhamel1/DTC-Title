# Virginia (VA) Title / Closing / Settlement Fee Evidence

## Status: complete (scarce) — 5 verified sources, 2026-07-21

Virginia does not meet the target (10) or saturation (6+) bar, but meets the contract's
**scarce** completion criterion. An exhaustive search across roughly 20 distinct query
strategies and direct provider-site checks (well past the contract's 8-strategy
threshold) surfaced only 5 sources with genuine, verifiable, priced fee figures — 2
underwriter title-premium-only manuals (Stewart, WFG, both of which explicitly state
Virginia law/practice separates title premium from settlement/closing service fees,
consistent with VA Code §38.2-4608) and 3 settlement/title companies (Republic Title,
Federal Title & Escrow, Lighthouse Title). Numerous additional VA closing attorneys and
title companies (Quill, Cardinal Title Group, Weichert Title Agency, Mid-Atlantic Title,
Old Republic's VA escrow department) were found to exist and operate in the state but
publish no static fee schedule, requiring direct contact or a quote tool instead — a
genuine market-opacity finding. Marked **complete (scarce)**.

## Settlement fee range actually observed

Only 3 of the 5 sources price settlement/closing fees (the 2 underwriter manuals are
title-premium-only by VA statutory design):

- **Republic Title**: $595 (buyer, purchase or refinance) to $850 (seller, purchase) —
  core settlement fee only, plus title insurance premiums and itemized add-ons.
- **Federal Title & Escrow**: $1,275 (Arlington/Fairfax/Alexandria) to $1,475 (other VA
  counties) — a single all-inclusive figure covering title exam, storage, courier, tax
  certificate, recording, and notary.
- **Lighthouse Title** (2010 vintage): $410 (seller) to $775 (refinance, up to 2
  payoffs) per side for standard residential transactions.

**Observed range: roughly $410 (Lighthouse seller, 2010) to $1,475 (Federal Title,
current, other-counties tier)** — a wide spread partly explained by date (Lighthouse's
figures are 15 years old) and partly by bundling scope (Federal Title's figure is a
single all-inclusive number covering services Republic Title itemizes separately).
Notably, Federal Title's fee is *higher* in "other VA counties" than in the
Arlington/Fairfax/Alexandria core — the reverse of the usual urban-premium pattern seen
in most other states' settlement fees, worth flagging as a genuine VA-specific finding
rather than a data error (confirmed by direct quote from the source page).

## Itemization / bundling patterns

- **Underwriters strictly separate premium from settlement fees.** Both Stewart and WFG
  explicitly disclaim settlement/closing/escrow charges in their rate manuals, directly
  citing the VA statutory framework (VA Code §38.2-4608) that requires title insurance
  risk rates to exclude escrow/closing/settlement services — the clearest explicit
  statutory citation for this pattern found in any state surveyed to date (more explicit
  than GA's attorney-close convention or CA's practice-based separation).
- **CPL pricing diverges more than in other states**: Stewart $50/letter vs. WFG
  $35/letter — a ~43% difference between the two underwriters, a larger CPL spread than
  the tight $45-50 clustering observed in Georgia.
- **Settlement companies bundle differently**: Federal Title publishes one fully
  bundled all-inclusive number; Republic Title keeps settlement fee, deed prep, and
  trust review as separate line items; Lighthouse Title bundles settlement fee + courier
  + title exam + title binder into one per-side "Title Services" figure but keeps deed
  prep and release tracking separate — three distinct bundling philosophies from three
  independent VA companies, more variation in bundling approach than seen in most other
  states.
- **Buyer/seller fee split is explicit and asymmetric** in both Republic Title ($595
  buyer / $850 seller) and Lighthouse Title ($575 buyer / $410 seller) — sellers pay
  more at Republic (likely reflecting deed prep and payoff/release handling), buyers pay
  more at Lighthouse (likely reflecting title exam and binder inclusion) — underscoring
  that "who pays what" and "what's bundled where" vary meaningfully by company even
  within the same state.

## Calculator harvest addendum (2026-07-26)

Separate from the published-schedule survey above (which remains **complete (scarce
market)**), this session harvested three provider quote calculators for the standard
$500,000/$400,000 Fairfax, VA purchase scenario — see VA.json entries marked `"basis":
"calculator"` and CALCULATORS.md for full technical detail:

1. **Bon Air Title Agency** (`bonairtitle.com`, Richmond, VA) — embeds a per-agency
   instance of the TitleClose.com white-label consumer platform
   (`bonairtitleagency.titleclose.com`), driven via a plain ASP.NET MVC POST/GET flow
   with no JS execution and no personal data required. Itemizes CPL $50, Search Fee
   $165, Binder Fee $75, and Recording $100, but explicitly zeroes its own "Settlement,
   Closing or Escrow Fee" line item ($0.00) — its own disclaimer confirms quotes are for
   title insurance only, with settlement handled by a separate outside closing attorney.
   Buyer-side total: $3,108.00.
2. **Appomattox** (Chesterfield, VA) — a second, distinct agency tenant on the same
   TitleClose.com platform (`apptitle.titleclose.com`), confirming the platform reflects
   each tenant's own real fee schedule: this agency charges a $450 Settlement/Closing/
   Escrow Fee (vs. Bon Air's $0), plus Admin Fee $375, Attorney Fee for Deed Docs $150,
   Title Commitment Fee $125, and separate buyer/seller Courier and Wire fees — the
   richest ancillary-fee breakdown of the two TitleClose instances. Buyer-side total:
   $3,718.00; seller-side fees: $670.00.
3. **Independent Title & Escrow LLC** (Fairfax, VA) — embeds a NetSheetCalc/TitleTap
   white-label net-sheet calculator (`app.netsheetcalc.com`, app_id 533/534, tenant
   `independent-VA`); this agency's Fairfax office address is an exact match to the
   standard scenario's target county, no substitution needed. Its own unauthenticated
   `getAppData` JSON endpoint exposes 10 hardcoded flat-dollar settlement/service fee
   constants directly (Settlement Fee $375, Abstract Fee $350, Title Binder $300, Exam
   Fee $300, Document Prep $300, CPL $50, E-Record $10, Deed Recording $47, Mortgage
   Recording $60), plus VA state/local grantee (recordation) tax formulas on both deed
   and mortgage; a separate live rate-API call (`api/index.php/rate/500000/Owner533`)
   returned this agency's own Owner's Title Insurance Premium of $2,097.50.

This crosses the 3-provider calculator-quoted threshold for VA. Two independent shared
platforms were newly discovered this session and are not yet logged in CALCULATORS.md:
**TitleClose.com** (a national ASP.NET MVC "shopping mall" SaaS, confirmed here across
2 distinct VA agency tenants, analogous in importance to MyTitleRates.com and
TitleCapture for other states) and **NetSheetCalc/TitleTap** (a Laravel-style JSON-API
SaaS product with a "Quick Quote, no sign-in needed" mode that exposes hardcoded
per-agency fee constants directly in an unauthenticated config endpoint). Both platforms
are used by many independent title agencies nationwide per their own marketing pages and
are likely high-leverage, multi-state/multi-agency unlocks for future harvest sessions,
following the same per-tenant-distinct-fee-schedule pattern already established for
MyTitleRates.com and TitleCapture elsewhere in CALCULATORS.md.
